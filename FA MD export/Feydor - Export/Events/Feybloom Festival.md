## Description & History

For the Thaylmorites, this fleeting moment of celebration is a rarity, a bloom of joy amidst a landscape shaped by loss. The period begins with the awakening of the feybloom outcropping just northwest of the Memorial to the Tree Keeper. There, the vibrant flowers unfold their petals in radiant display, heralding the arrival of Feydor’s temperate summer.&nbsp;&nbsp;<div><br></div><div>It is during this time that the Tree in Thaylmor, along with any trees blessed by the Keepers, bursts forth in beauty. The dark red maple leaves of feybloom emerge, stark and brilliant against the greens of the surrounding forest. Unlike the invasive vines that often choke the blooms elsewhere, these leaves flourish freely, a testament to the magic imbued by the Keepers.&nbsp;&nbsp;</div><div><br></div><div>The event transforms the air of Thaylmor. Laughter and song fill its streets, muted grief temporarily set aside as citizens gather to marvel at the rare beauty of the bloom. For many, the sight of the red maple leaves, glowing faintly under the summer sun, serves as a reminder of resilience and renewal—a chance to embrace hope, however fleeting, in the shadow of the Fall.&nbsp;&nbsp;</div><div><br></div><div>This is their season, their gaiety, their defiance against despair. For the feybloom does not just mark the passage of time—it declares that even amid ruin, life can flourish.</div>
