## Description & History


The monument stands in quiet reverence, a solitary tombstone rising amidst the clearing. It is unassuming, yet its presence carries weight—a testament to the queen’s sacrifice after the fall of Feydor at the hands of the Lich.
<div>Feydorian feybloom winds across the stone, its delicate petals whispering of the homeland she could not save. Strangely, High Forest feybloom grows alongside it, intertwining as though honoring both her legacy and the resilience of those who fought beside her.
</div><div>Nearby, an outcropping of Feydorian feybloom blooms in abundance, its vibrant colors filling the space with life. The clearing feels untouched, sacred, as if shaped by forces unseen to serve as a tribute to its caretaker. The air is still, the beauty undeniable, a fitting reflection of the queen’s enduring spirit

    <p>The Pilgrimage to the Monument of the Queen is a sacred journey undertaken by the citizens of Thaylmor and nearby regions to honor the memory of the Feydorian queen. Located just off the Royal Path through the forest, the Monument stands as a simple yet profound testament to the queen’s sacrifice following the fall of Feydor to the Lich.</p>

    

    <p>The pilgrimage began shortly after the establishment of the Monument and has since become a cherished tradition. The queen’s actions, which preserved the remnants of Feydor and protected its people, are remembered through this annual journey. As a cultural practice, it unites Thaylmorites in grief, reverence, and hope, symbolizing their shared connection to their homeland and its history.</p>





    <p>Though the Monument’s location is relatively close to Thaylmor, the act of pilgrimage holds deep symbolic meaning. Pilgrims travel the Royal Path with solemnity, reflecting on the queen’s sacrifice. They carry offerings such as sprigs of Feydorian or High Forest feybloom, small tokens, or handwritten prayers to lay at the site.</p>

    

    <p>The journey culminates in the Monument’s clearing, where the tombstone stands adorned with intertwined Feydorian and High Forest feybloom. Here, pilgrims kneel, lay their offerings, and recite prayers. Some spend extended moments in silent reflection, drawing strength from the sacred surroundings before returning home.</p>





    <p>The Monument is a solitary tombstone surrounded by a serene clearing just off the Royal Path. Feydorian and High Forest feybloom grow across the stone, their vibrant colors serving as a living reminder of unity and resilience. The forest canopy filters sunlight gently onto the site, imbuing it with an almost otherworldly peace. An outcropping of Feydorian feybloom nearby blooms in wild abundance, further enhancing the Monument’s sacred aura.</p>

    

    <p>The pilgrimage remains a vital tradition for Thaylmorites, passed down through generations. It serves not only as a tribute to the queen but also as a means of personal renewal for those who undertake the journey. Pilgrims often return with a deeper sense of connection to Feydor’s history and the enduring strength of its people.</p>
