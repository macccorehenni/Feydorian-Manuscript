## Traditions & Customs

Each year, as the Feybloom Festival begins, the gates—normally closed to preserve the sanctity of their surroundings—are flung wide to welcome the people. The grounds, steeped in history and touched by the magic of feybloom, transform into the heart of the celebration. For this brief time, the sacred and the communal intertwine, creating a space where laughter, music, and the vibrant petals of feybloom bloom together under the temperate summer sky.
