## Description & History

&nbsp; &nbsp; <title>Thaylmor, Capital of Feydor</title>&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;<br>&nbsp; &nbsp; Thaylmor stretches endlessly, a city of grandeur and sorrow that takes 16 hours to cross on foot. Its skyline rises in defiance of the Lich’s grasp, the stone spires and towering keeps seemingly untouched by the corruption spreading across the kingdom. The streets are a labyrinth, winding through districts that reflect every part of Feydor’s once-thriving soul.<br>

&nbsp; &nbsp; <h2>The Heart of the City</h2>

&nbsp; &nbsp; <p>At its center lies the Grand Citadel, its high walls gleaming with enchantments older than most kingdoms. The Feydorian feybloom grows in carefully cultivated gardens here, the petals glimmering faintly in the shadows, a reminder of the kingdom’s heritage. Beneath the Citadel, the markets pulse with life—merchants hawking their wares, fey whispering secrets for coins infused with magic, and weary travelers weaving through the crowds.</p>

&nbsp; &nbsp; <h2>The Outer Districts</h2>

&nbsp; &nbsp; <p>The outer districts of Thaylmor hold the remnants of hope. Here, houses crafted of timber and stone sit close together, their chimneys rising into the overcast skies. Lanterns sway above cobbled streets, casting warm glows against the fading light. Artisans work tirelessly, their craft keeping Feydor alive even in its darkest moments.</p>

&nbsp; &nbsp; <h2>The Passageways</h2>

&nbsp; &nbsp; <p>To cross Thaylmor is to journey through layers of history and emotion. Narrow alleyways carved from ancient stone weave between grand avenues lined with statues of long-forgotten heroes. The paths seem endless, branching toward the unknown, as if the city itself breathes, alive and watching. Fountains adorned with intricate carvings mark crossroads, their waters infused with faint traces of Feydorian magic.</p>

&nbsp; &nbsp; <h2>The People</h2>

&nbsp; &nbsp; <p>The citizens of Thaylmor walk with purpose, their resolve tempered by fear. Many wear tokens of protection—pendants etched with runes, small bundles of Feydorian herbs pinned to cloaks. Despite the looming threat, there is resilience in their expressions, a quiet defiance that refuses to be extinguished.</p>

&nbsp; &nbsp; <h2>The Edge of the City</h2>

&nbsp; &nbsp; <p>The edges of Thaylmor feel different. Here, where the towering spires give way to farmlands and open fields, the air carries the faintest trace of the Lich’s corruption. The vibrant green fades, replaced by shadows that linger too long and whispers that chill the skin. Even here, the city endures, holding the line against the encroaching darkness.</p>

&nbsp; &nbsp; <p>Thaylmor is not simply a city—it is the beating heart of Feydor, a place where history, magic, and the will of its people intertwine. To cross it is to feel the weight of its legacy, the hope and the sorrow standing side by side.</p>
